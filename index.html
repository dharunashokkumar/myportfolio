inside the homelab

an architecture sketch of the systems i work with. open a layer to explore its job and the engineering question behind it.

based on lpu labs and vmmanager; this is a learning map, not a live inventory or status dashboard.

linux hostcompute, networking, and storage underneath it all
01 · browser labs

runs: development environments managed through fastapi and docker, with browser access through kasmvnc or code-server.

why: make a prepared environment accessible without local installation.

the question: how do you reclaim idle compute without removing a user's work?

explore lpu labs →

02 · virtual machines

runs: qemu/kvm virtual machines managed through libvirt; incus containers through a separate api.

why: keep both management paths in one native desktop interface.

the lesson: a quiet dashboard hides memory ownership and connections that can fail.

read the field note →

03 · networks & volumes

runs: per-user docker networks and persistent volumes in the lab platform.

why: separate users' traffic and keep files beyond a container's lifetime.

the question: which resources belong to a session, and which belong to the user?

read the tradeoffs →

04 · observability

runs: prometheus metrics, structured logs, and container health checks in the documented lab architecture.

why: make resource usage and lifecycle events inspectable.

the question: can the logs explain a failed launch without reproducing it?
