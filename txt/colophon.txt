colophon
========

hand-written html, css and a little javascript. no framework, no build
step, no dependencies. the repository is the website. view source; it's
meant to be read.

type: merriweather, self-hosted. lowercase. greys, plus a highlighter
yellow and a gold. one shadow. almost no animation.

what a page weighs (gzip -9, from the repository)

party, animate for decoration, or need javascript to be read.
