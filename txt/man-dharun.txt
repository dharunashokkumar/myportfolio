NAME
       dharun - builds systems software, runs d's labs

SYNOPSIS
       dharun [--rust | --go | --c] [--self-host] problem

DESCRIPTION
       dharun ashokkumar builds systems software in rust, go and c, and
       runs d's labs: browser lab environments served from a server at
       home.

       started with arduino at school, then raspberry pi and esp32,
       then servers and the software around them. studies computer
       science at lovely professional university, punjab, 2025 to 2029.
       was a development intern at pantech prolabs, october 2024 to
       january 2025.

OPTIONS
       --rust     clearing engine, rler
       --go       termcall
       --c        vmmanager
       --self-host
                  d's labs and about thirty services on one home server

FILES
       /homelab   the machine
       /now       this month
       /uses      the tools

BUGS
       chooses the longer route on purpose, when the point is to learn
       what the abstraction hides.
