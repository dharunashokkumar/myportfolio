the homelab
===========

one proxmox ve machine at my family's home, two thousand kilometres from
where i study. it runs d's labs and about thirty other guests.

live status isn't wired up yet. the incidents below are the honest
record.

the machine
-----------

cpu           intel core i9-14900k, capped at 90 w sustained, 125 w
              burst
board         gigabyte b760m ds3h, micro-atx
memory        32 gb ddr4-3200
disks         1 tb wd_black sn7100 nvme, 2 tb wd red plus
cooling       360 mm aio, plus a fan zip-tied over the power stage
power         corsair rm850e. no ups for the server.
network       2.5 gbe, home fibre, changing address, inbound web ports
              blocked

incidents, newest first
-----------------------

21 oct 2026   several power cuts in the week i moved out. it came back
              on its own every time.
oct 2026      the isp router dropped its port-forwarding rules until
              restarted.
oct 2026      a power cut seconds after a config write left a dns file
              empty.
oct 2026      the firewall silently broke tailscale access to the
              guests.
oct 2026      a stray command deleted /dev/null and ssh died with it.
oct 2026      cold boots took 13 minutes. now 22 guests are back in 53
              seconds.
